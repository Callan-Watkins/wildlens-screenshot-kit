# WildLens: what the app is

This folder has:
- `1-app-captures/`: real screenshots of the app running on iPhone (Simulator). Use these as the app screens.
- `2-current-app-store-screenshots/`: the App Store screenshots made earlier from those captures, in store order. They show what already exists; the new set can go in a different direction.

---

WildLens is a free iPhone app that identifies nature from a photo and tells you whether it belongs where you found it.

Who it's for: curious people outdoors, such as hikers, gardeners, families and kids, and anyone who wants to know "what is that?" about a plant, bug, bird, animal or mushroom.

What it does:
- Identify: snap or pick a photo and WildLens names the species in about a second, with its common and scientific name. An optional one-time download (about 97 MB) adds an on-device identifier covering about 16,800 species, including animals, insects, birds and fungi. With it, identification runs offline, unlimited and private, and the photo never leaves the phone. Without it, plants are identified in the cloud, up to 5 a day for free.
- Honest when unsure: when it isn't confident it says so. It shows the likely matches to pick from, can say "almost certainly a kind of…" when only the genus is clear, and lets you add up to two more photos of the same thing for a better answer.
- Range Check, its signature feature: tells you whether the species is native, introduced or invasive where you are, using real recorded sightings shown on a map. Only your rounded location is used.
- At risk?: conservation status from the IUCN Red List and regional sources.
- Safety warnings: flags toxic look-alikes and plants that burn the skin.
- Report invasives: send an invasive find to EDDMapS or iNaturalist straight from the result.
- Each species page also has: an About section from encyclopedia sources, its family tree and look-alikes, and fun facts from the web with every fact linked to its source. You can also ask questions about the species, answered by Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones.
- Collection: every find you save becomes a numbered collectible card with your photo, kept on the phone and viewable offline. There are also streaks, badges and a monthly challenge.
- Spot these near you: shows what's commonly recorded around you this month, so you can go find them.
- Nature walks: start a walk and see your time and finds on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
- Quick access: open the camera with one press from the Camera Control button, the Action button, Siri or Control Center, even from the Lock Screen on iOS 18.
- Share cards: share any find as a designed card with the photo, its name and whether it belongs. Your location is never shared.
- Available in 9 languages. Also adapts to the iPhone Duo's large inner screen.

Personality: playful and collectible, but trustworthy. The facts come from real scientific sources, never from AI guessing, and the app is upfront about uncertainty.

Tagline ideas already in use: "Snap it. Know it." / "Does it belong here?" / "Collect the wild."
